💡 What Is ReadytoWork?

ReadytoWork is a tray launcher for Windows. If you have never heard that term before, here is the plain-English version: it is a small program that lives in the notification area — the little strip of icons next to your clock in the bottom-right corner of the screen. Instead of opening the Start menu and scrolling, or squinting at twenty desktop shortcuts, you click the ReadytoWork icon and a tidy menu appears with the things you actually use.

Think of it as your own personal launchpad. You decide what goes in it. It could be your word processor, your college assignment folder, a spreadsheet you update every day, the school portal website, your email inbox, the printer settings page, or that PDF you keep opening again and again. Anything you can click, you can add.

Because it lives in the tray, ReadytoWork is always there but never in the way. It does not take over your screen, it does not pop up advertisements, and it does not need you to learn a new way of working. You click, something opens, and you get on with your day.
